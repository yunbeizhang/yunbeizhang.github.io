Yunbei Zhang

Final-year Ph.D. Candidate

I work on safe, adaptive, and self-evolving AI: world models and physical AI for multimodal and embodied reasoning, the safety and reliability of frontier models and agents, and efficient adaptation under distribution shift, limited compute, and black-box access.

I recently completed a research internship at Waymo, after internships at Amazon, Oak Ridge National Laboratory, and KLA. I studied mathematics at Osaka University and Peking University.

Open to research internships and collaborations: yunbeizhang.ml [at] gmail.com
