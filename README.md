## Editorial style

- Use a typographic apostrophe and two-digit year for conference names: `NeurIPS’26`, `ECCV’26`, `CVPR’26`, `ICML’25`, and `WACV’25`. Use `NeurIPS’25–’26` for year ranges.
- In publication labels, use `NeurIPS’26 Workshop`, `ICLRW’26`, and `CVPRW’26`. News may retain specific workshop names, such as `NeurIPS’26 Robotics World Modeling Workshop`.
- Keep workshop names in normal weight without venue hyperlinks. Paper names may link to the paper or project, and award badges retain their emphasis.
- Keep calendar dates, education and employment periods, and preprint or under-review years in their existing formats.
